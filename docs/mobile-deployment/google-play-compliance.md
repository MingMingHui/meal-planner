# Google Play compliance checklist

Audit of the Android app (`com.ch2digital.mealplannerai`) against Google Play requirements, as of **2026-11-23**. A successful build is **not** evidence of policy compliance; this document records what the repository actually does and what still needs a human.

**Legend.** Status: ✅ done in repo · ⚠️ partial / needs confirmation · ❌ not done (**BLOCKER** = must be resolved before a production release) · ➖ not applicable.
Policies change. Re-check each item against the live [Policy Center](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/topic/9858052) and the Play Console's own prompts before submitting.

## Production blockers (summary)

1. **Privacy policy**: none exists. Needs a public URL written or approved by the owner/legal (item 1).
2. **Supabase redirect URL** for the app's deep link isn't registered yet, so sign-in from the app won't return to it (item 13).
3. **AI-generated content reporting**: no in-app way to report offensive AI output (item 21).
4. **Real upload key**: the only AAB built so far was signed with a throwaway test key (item 25).
5. **Data safety, health apps, content rating, target audience** declarations must be completed in Play Console (items 3, 4, 10, 11, 12).
6. **12 testers × 14 days** closed test, if the developer account is a personal account created after 13 Nov 2023 (item 26).
