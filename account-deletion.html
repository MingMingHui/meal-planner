Delete your Health Meal Planning Agent account

You can delete your account and its data yourself, at any time, from the Android app or from this website.

Steps

  1. Open the app (or this website) and sign in with the same Gmail or Outlook account you used before.
  2. Go to Settings → Your data.
  3. Tap Delete Account and type DELETE to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account.
  • All data stored in your account in the cloud: profile, meal plans, saved recipes, shopping lists, progress entries (weight, calories, water), achievements and app settings.
  • The copy of that data on the device you delete from.

Deletion is immediate and permanent; it cannot be undone. If you sign in again later with the same email address, you start with a new, empty account.

What is not affected

  • Files you exported yourself (JSON, CSV, PDF) stay wherever you saved them.
  • Data on other devices that are signed in to the account remains on those devices until you clear it there (Settings → Delete Local Data) or uninstall the app.
  • Information you chose to send to an AI provider (when using the Recipe Generator, AI Meal Plan or AI Coach with your own API key) is held by that provider under its own privacy policy.

Guest users

Guest sessions have no account. Guest data exists only on your device for the current session and is removed when the session ends, or immediately with Settings → Delete Local Data.
